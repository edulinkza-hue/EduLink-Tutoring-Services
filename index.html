About EduLink

EduLink is an academic support platform dedicated to connecting students with the right tutors to help them succeed.

We support high school and university students who need structured, reliable, and personalized academic assistance — from concept clarification to exam preparation.

What sets EduLink apart is our personalized tutor-matching process. Each student is paired with a tutor whose academic strengths, teaching style, and subject expertise best fit their needs.

All EduLink tutors are carefully vetted, academically strong, and committed to helping students achieve measurable academic improvement.

Our Vision To become a trusted academic support platform that empowers students to reach their full potential through personalized learning and quality tutoring.

At EduLink, we provide personalised tutoring for both high school and university students, with a strong focus on University of Pretoria (UP) curricula and expectations.

  • Natural & Agricultural Sciences (NAS): Mathematics, Mathematical Statistics, and Statistics support including calculus, probability, regression analysis, and quantitative problem-solving — and many more.
  • Economic & Management Sciences (EMS): Accounting, Financial Management, Investments, and Economics support including financial analysis, economic theory, and quantitative business concepts.
  • Engineering, Built Environment & IT (EBIT): University-level mathematics, applied mathematics, engineering mathematics, programming fundamentals, and analytical problem-solving aligned with UP’s engineering and IT curriculum.

Whether you are preparing for university, adjusting to your first year at UP, or aiming to improve your results, our tutors understand the academic standards and pressures you face — and guide you with confidence.
